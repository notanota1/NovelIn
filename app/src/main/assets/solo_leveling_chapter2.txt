The high-stakes survival at the Cartenon Temple reaches its climax as Sung Jin-Woo uses his sharp observation skills to decipher the final commandment while the remaining hunters are being slaughtered by the terrifying stone statues. After completing the trial, Jin-Woo is left behind and mortally wounded on the altar, facing certain death as the statues close in for the final blow. However, just as his life is about to end, a mysterious system notification appears before him, offering him the chance to become a "Player." In his final moments of consciousness, Jin-Woo accepts the mysterious contract of the "System," triggering a complete transformation that saves his life and marks the beginning of his journey from the world's weakest hunter to an entity with the unique ability to level up.
