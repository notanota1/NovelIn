King Grey, a powerful and unparalleled ruler in a world governed by martial ability, who nonetheless lives a life of profound isolation and emotional emptiness despite his immense status. Upon his sudden and mysterious death, he is shocked to find himself reborn as a helpless infant named Arthur Leywin in a vibrant new world filled with magic and wonder. Retaining the vast knowledge and disciplined mind of his previous life, Arthur quickly realizes that this world operates on the laws of "Mana" rather than the "Ki" he once mastered. The chapter highlights his internal transition as he resolves to live this new life differently, seeking the warmth of family and genuine connections that he lacked as a king, all while beginning his secret training to harness the magical potential of his new body.
