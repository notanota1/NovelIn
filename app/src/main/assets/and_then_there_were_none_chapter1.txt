Justice Wargrave, Vera Claythorne, Philip Lombard, Emily Brent, General Macarthur, Dr. Armstrong, Tony Marston, and Mr. Blore—as they travel by train or car toward a mysterious destination called Soldier Island off the coast of Devon. Each character has been lured there under different pretexts, ranging from a summer job or a medical consultation to a simple invitation from an old friend, yet they all share a sense of curiosity about their elusive host, Mr. U.N. Owen. As they journey, the narrative subtly reveals glimpses of their diverse social backgrounds and hidden anxieties, building an atmosphere of suspense that culminates when they finally gather at the shoreline, oblivious to the fact that their shared presence on the isolated island is part of a dark and calculated scheme.
