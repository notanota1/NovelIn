Walter Hartright, a drawing master who secures a position at Limmeridge House through the help of his friend, Professor Pesca. However, on the night before his departure, the atmosphere turns chilling when he encounters a mysterious woman dressed entirely in white on a lonely road in Hampstead. The woman appears terrified, asks for directions to London, and mentions her deep affection for the Fairlie family in Cumberland before Walter eventually helps her escape. Shortly after she disappears, Walter is startled by a group of men searching for her, who reveal the shocking fact that she has just escaped from a lunatic asylum, leaving Walter with a sense of dread and a lingering feeling that he has just stepped into a dark mystery.
